Plastic Identification Reference

Five resin types in this simulator and familiar product examples in Indonesia
Plastic typeResin codeRecycling symbolEveryday product examples
Polyethylene terephthalate (PET)1 — PETClear disposable mineral-water bottles; ready-to-drink bottled tea or soft-drink bottles.
High-density polyethylene (HDPE)2 — HDPEOpaque laundry-detergent bottles; rigid shampoo bottles; some sturdy cooking-oil jerrycans.
Polyvinyl chloride (PVC)3 — PVCHousehold water-supply or drain pipes; flexible insulation around electrical cables.
Low-density polyethylene (LDPE)4 — LDPESoft clear bread bags; some thin produce bags used at traditional markets; flexible plastic wrapping.
Polypropylene (PP)5 — PPTakeaway-food containers; reusable lunchboxes; many flip-top bottle caps.

Examples are indicative: check the resin marking on the actual product. A bottle, cap, and label can use different plastics; the symbol does not guarantee local recycling acceptance. PVC and PET are shown for identification, not as recommended pyrolysis feedstocks.
